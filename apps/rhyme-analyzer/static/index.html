How it works: each word is looked up in the CMU Pronouncing Dictionary → its rime (phonemes from the last stressed vowel). Words with the same rime are a perfect rhyme family (one color). With "slant" on, words sharing just the stressed vowel are grouped too (assonance / near rhymes). Hover a highlighted word to see its rhyme sound. Words not in the dictionary fall back to spelling, so slang may be approximate.
