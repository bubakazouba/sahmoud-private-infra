Based on Damodaran 1928-2024 real US equity returns — live Monte Carlo, no fabricated data  |  Updated 2026-05-05

97-year empirical bootstrap  ·  no parametric fits i We don't assume returns follow a bell curve or any other formula. We resample from the actual 97 historical years directly. This captures fat tails (extreme crashes/booms happen more often than a normal distribution predicts) without us having to model them.
▶ How to read this dashboard
  1. Adjust sliders at the top — every chart re-renders live as you drag.
  2. All dollar values are REAL (constant 2026 purchasing power). A "$80k/yr spend" means $80k of today's buying power every year — the actual dollar amount you'd spend grows with inflation, but we track it in today's dollars throughout.
  3. Returns are bootstrapped from real (inflation-adjusted) US equity history 1928–2024 (Damodaran S&P 500 incl. dividends + FRED CPI). No synthetic distribution is assumed.
  4. We are NOT modeling: taxes, glide-path away from 100% equities, dynamic withdrawal strategy beyond constant real spend, healthcare costs, or partner income.
  5. Hover or tap any i icon for a plain-English explanation of that term.
