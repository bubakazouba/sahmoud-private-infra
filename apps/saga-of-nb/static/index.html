The sequence diagram

Three NB entities. Two BBB phases. One customer doing all the orchestration.
act i · Feb 6, 2026

The Purchase happy

Sahmoud walks into Run With Us at 235 N Lake Ave, Pasadena. Maria rings up an M86014D in size 12.5 D for $139.99 plus $14.70 tax. Total: $154.69 on Visa 5590. The shoes are beautiful. The shoes will run many miles.
act ii · ~March 2026

The Hole

After under 2 months of normal use, a hole appears through the upper mesh. Not the sole. Not the laces. The mesh. Above the toes. As if the shoe was actively trying to breathe.
act iii · early April

"Talk to New Balance" classic

Sahmoud returns to Run With Us. Run With Us, the authorized New Balance retailer, suggests Sahmoud talk to New Balance. The buck is officially passed.
act iv · early April · plot twist

...Australia? geography

Sahmoud contacts New Balance customer service from his computer in Pasadena, California. Through some routing alchemy unknown to mortals, his ticket lands in the inbox of New Balance Australia. Cases #781506 and #780688 are opened. The Pacific Ocean was not consulted.
act v · mid-April

The 20% Goodwill Voucher

NB Australia's offer: a 20% off goodwill voucher. Toward a future NB purchase. From the company that made the shoe that has a hole in it. Sahmoud declines.
act vi · April 13

The BBB Files

Sahmoud files BBB complaint #24745800 against New Balance Athletics, Inc. Desired settlement: refund. The Better Business Bureau has been notified.
act vii · April 27 · the gaslight

"The customer lives in Australia" galaxy brain

NB's response via BBB:
"The customer lives in Australia and has reached our US-based website, but we are only able to assist with US inquiries."
The customer does not live in Australia. The customer lives in Pasadena. The Australian cases were opened by NB themselves, by accident, three weeks ago. New Balance is now using their own routing error as grounds to dismiss the complaint.
act viii · April 28 · the cavalry

Tim from Corporate a hero arrives

Tim, Customer Experience Associate at the New Balance corporate office in Boston, USA, sends an email:
"I apologize for any confusion regarding the Australian team you have been communicating with. We do not have any access into your communication with them."
Tim asks for photos and proof of purchase. Sahmoud sends 12 shoe photos and the receipt. Hope flickers.
act ix · April 28 · the response

Reject Business Response

Sahmoud rejects NB's BBB-side dismissal, citing Tim's email as evidence that NB US has explicitly taken jurisdiction. The two parallel arms of New Balance — BBB-facing and corporate-facing — have, at this point, told entirely different stories about whether Sahmoud is from Australia.
act x · April 29 · Tim returns

The Verdict surprise

Tim, having reviewed the 12 photos, the receipt, the case numbers, and the timeline, delivers his findings:
"While the purchase was made recently, the wear on the outsoles and midsoles do indicate the shoes have been heavily worn in that timeframe."
The hole, you see, is in the upper mesh. Not the outsole. Not the midsole. Tim's reply does not mention the upper mesh.
