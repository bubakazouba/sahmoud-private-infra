Expense Dashboard

Re-categorized from raw Empower transaction data (not Empower's own rollups)
Method: downloaded all ~11K raw transactions from Empower's getUserTransactions API, then re-bucketed by my own rules (NOT trusting Empower's categories).

Excluded as "not real spend": account-to-account TRANSFERS, Securities Trades (fund rebalances, 401k moves), Credit Card Payments (the underlying merchant txs are already counted, so the CC bill payment is a shadow), Deposits, Investment Income, Paychecks.

Net effect: his REAL spend is meaningfully different from Empower's number because Empower's "moneyOut" treats some borderline things differently. See the YoY comparison below.
Reimbursements deep-dive. Money that came back IN to you (work expense reimbursements + people venmo/zelle-ing you) — these subtract from your spend totals on the other tabs.
Checking-only view. Spend out of Total Checking - 0833 minus reimbursements that came back in. Excludes Vanguard buys + taxes (per same rules as Tab 1).
Per card drill-down. Every account Empower sees, by year. Click a card to expand: monthly trend, top merchants, full tx list (with text filter).
2025 vs 2026 deep dive. Headline cards annualize 2026 (×12/5) so you can compare to a full year. The merchant tables below use Jan–May vs Jan–May so one-off purchases (furniture, donations) show their real $ — not 2.4×-inflated. The checking-only method is the more accurate top-line since it captures all CC payments.
