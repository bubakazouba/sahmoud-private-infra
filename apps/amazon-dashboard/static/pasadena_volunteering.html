Pasadena Volunteering — Show Up, No Commitment

One-time / drop-in volunteering near home (Pasadena, 91106). Researched June 2026. Sorted by how truly no-strings each one is.
Bottom line on your two questions:  •  Skills? Essentially none required for every option below — they hand you a task and walk you through it.  •  One-time OK? The green ones you can literally show up (or pick a date) with zero ongoing commitment. The amber ones need one quick one-time orientation, then you drop in whenever.
