Reading the data. Prices/durations are the published beginner figures as of 6/15/2026; "call" = the school doesn't publish it online (you'd have to phone). Reviews: Google/Yelp star ratings are hard to scrape reliably (Yelp blocks bots), so where a platform's star couldn't be verified we show the review count or an alternate source (TripAdvisor/Birdeye/Nicelocal) and label it — nothing here is made up. "ASA" and "US Sailing" are the two main US cert bodies (both nationally recognized for renting; ASA has a slight edge for chartering yachts abroad). Keelboat = stable big boat you'd cruise/charter; dinghy = small tippy boat, more athletic + a swim/capsize test.
