Notes — what's confirmed vs estimated, and sources

Confirmed (comma-published / Wikipedia / press):

  • Founded Sept 2015 by George Hotz; openpilot open-sourced Nov 30 2016. confirmed
  • Miles: ~25M (2020), >90M (2023), 300M+ (current, 2025–26). confirmed
  • Users: 6,000+ (2023) → 20,000+ (current). confirmed
  • Supported cars: grew to 250+, then 300+, now 325+. confirmed
  • Devices & launch prices: comma one (2016, $999), EON devkit (2017, $699), comma two (2020, $999), comma three (2021, $2,199), comma 3X (2023, $1,250), comma four (2025, $999). confirmed
  • Funding: $3.1M (2016, Andreessen Horowitz), $5M (2018), ~$18.1M total across 3 rounds (latest Nov 2021). confirmed
  • Headcount: 11–50 employees (2024, aggregator data). reported

Estimated / not disclosed (shown dashed or grey):

  • Device units sold: comma does NOT publish sales. Only one figure was ever stated: ~5,000 comma 3 units by Jan 2023. The cumulative-units line is an estimate extrapolated from that point + the 20k-user base — illustrative only.
  • Intermediate mile milestones between the published 25M / 90M / 300M points are interpolated for the trend line. interp
  • Valuation / revenue: not publicly disclosed (private company, no IPO). Pre-IPO marketplaces list it but no official figure exists.
