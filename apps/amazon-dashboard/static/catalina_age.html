The island skews older, and the young-adult bracket is thin. Only 17% are 18-34 (well below the US ~23%), while 24% are 65+ (vs US ~17%). That dip in young adults lines up with the earlier point: graduates tend to leave for mainland college and jobs, so the island ages.
Source: U.S. Census ACS via DataUSA (shares are of the insured population, ~96.7% of residents, effectively the whole population). Median age from California Demographics / Census. Brackets shown are the 4 published groups; a finer 5-year age breakdown would need a direct Census ACS pull.
