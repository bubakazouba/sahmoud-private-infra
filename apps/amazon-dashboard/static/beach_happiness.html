🏖️ Does being by the beach make people happier? Yes — the most, of anywhere.

Real numbers from MacKerron & Mourato (2013), "Happiness is greater in natural environments" — the Mappiness study, 1,138,481 phone check-ins from 21,947 people in the UK, GPS-tagged to the exact spot. Bars = how many happiness points each setting adds vs. a city, on a 0–100 scale, after controlling for weather, who you're with, what you're doing, time of day, and the person themselves.
