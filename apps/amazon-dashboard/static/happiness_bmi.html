🙂 Happiness / Life-Satisfaction vs BMI & Body Fat

Synthesis of peer-reviewed studies (UK Biobank MR, Swiss & Finnish panels, BODY-Q, NHANES). The short answer: real but WEAK, nonlinear, and gender-dependent.
