Events

The full programme of tastings, symposia, master classes, and competitive events. From the annual flagship Grand Buckwheat Festival to intimate vertical flights for twelve, each event is curated by the Buckwheat Spectator panel.

Jun 6 2026
Grand Buckwheat Festival — 38th Annual Edition
Black Tie  ·  Estienne-Beauvoir Estate, Upper Ojai CA  ·  200 Guests Maximum

The flagship event of the Buckwheat Spectator year. Three days on the 47-acre estate at Upper Ojai, encompassing the Spectator Awards Gala, a guided vertical of the Mancan Reserve 2014–2019, an open tasting of sixty cultivars from nine appellations, master classes with all five panel critics, and a Saturday evening dinner prepared by a chef of the first rank, served in the restored 1907 craftsman lodge. Day passes, full weekend packages, and Patron allocations available.

16:30 — Open tasting; all 60 cultivars available

Jun 14 2026
Allegheny Vertical: Mancan Reserve 2014–2019
Smart Casual  ·  The Penn State Creamery, University Park PA  ·  24 Guests

A rare opportunity to evaluate six consecutive vintages of the Domaine Mancan Reserve in a controlled vertical format, guided by Patrick Czernowitz-Hale ABS. The 2019 vintage, at 98 points, represents the apex of the Allegheny appellation's recent history; evaluating it in context of its immediate predecessors provides critical insight unavailable from any single-vintage tasting. Allocation strictly limited; registration by ballot for non-Subscriber members.

$485 per person

California Appellation Inaugural Symposium
Smart Casual  ·  Estienne-Beauvoir Estate, Upper Ojai CA  ·  40 Guests

The inaugural dedicated symposium for the California buckwheat appellation, presented by Margaux Estienne-Beauvoir and featuring producers from the Upper Ojai and Santa Barbara appellations. Four current-vintage cultivars assessed alongside the Estienne-Beauvoir Estate Reserve, with discussion of the California terroir's claim to a position among the first tier of international production. A working symposium; active participation expected.

$320 per person

CBS Intensive Weekend — Dijon Campus
Casual  ·  Institut Fagopyrum, Dijon  ·  16 Guests Maximum

An intensive weekend covering the complete CBS curriculum, designed for candidates who require concentrated study in advance of the ABS examination. Both written components and all tasting sessions are included. Conducted in English with French interpretation available. Examination preparation material distributed on arrival.

Aug 8 2026
Harvest Observation Visit: Hokkaido Plateau
Casual Agricultural  ·  Central Hokkaido, Japan  ·  8 Guests Maximum

Join Yuki Nakamura-Estienne MBA for a four-day harvest observation programme at three Hokkaido estates, including the Koto Single-Origin farm. Guests will participate in supervised harvest tasting — evaluating groats immediately post-harvest before milling — observe the milling process, and attend two formal tasting sessions at the Hokkaido Groat Academy in Sapporo. A once-in-a-discipline opportunity. Accommodation provided at a Sapporo hotel of distinction.

$3,200 per person (accommodation included)

ABS Examination — Penn State Sitting
Formal Dress  ·  Penn State University, University Park PA  ·  By Registration

The annual North American sitting of the Associate Buckwheat Sommelier examination. Candidates must hold the CBS and have completed the required self-directed study programme. Registration closes 60 days before the examination date; late entries are not accepted under any circumstances. Results issued within 30 days by post.

$4,500 examination fee
Sep 26 2026
Volga Basin Estate Tour: Petrovsky & Solberg-Ostrowski
Casual  ·  Saratov Oblast, Russia  ·  10 Guests Maximum

Henrik Solberg-Ostrowski MB leads a five-day tour of the Saratov and Tambov growing regions, visiting the Petrovsky estate and four secondary producers. The tour includes formal tastings, soil assessment sessions, and a harvest dinner at the Petrovsky family's estate house. A working visit; geological interest an asset.

$2,800 per person

Concours International de Fagopyrum — 38th Annual
Black Tie (Awards Evening)  ·  Estienne-Beauvoir Estate, Upper Ojai CA  ·  Producer Submission Required

The foremost competitive forum for buckwheat producers worldwide. Three days of blind tastings before a panel of 18 Master Buckwheat Sommeliers, culminating in the awarding of the Grand Prix Estienne-Beauvoir on the evening of October 19th. Producer entries close August 1, 2026. Tickets for the awards evening available to non-producers from October 1st.

Hokkaido Master Class: Koto & Koban, Single-Region Afternoon
Smart Casual  ·  Buckwheat Academy, Sapporo  ·  18 Guests Maximum

An afternoon dedicated exclusively to the Niigata and Hokkaido appellations, led by Yuki Nakamura-Estienne MBA and two visiting Masters from the Kyoto School. Four vintages of Koto (2019–2022) will be assessed alongside three vintages of Koban (2021–2023). A light kaiseki meal is served between flights.

Nov 22 2026
Terroir Symposium: Pennsylvania Dutch Country vs. Volga Basin
Lounge Suit  ·  The Athenaeum, Philadelphia  ·  60 Guests

The proposition this year: "That the alluvial minerality of the Volga basin represents a fundamentally distinct aesthetic register from the limestone-driven structure of the Allegheny Plateau." Panellists include Margaux Estienne-Beauvoir, Henrik Solberg-Ostrowski, and two guest critics from the Institut Fagopyrum. A formal dinner follows the debate.

$390 per person

Mar 7 2027
Master of Buckwheat Examination — Hokkaido Sitting (Biennial)
Formal Dress  ·  Hokkaido Groat Academy, Sapporo  ·  By Invitation Only

The biennial Master of Buckwheat examination. Forty cultivars across seventeen appellations will be presented blind over three consecutive days. Candidates must hold the Buckwheat Professional designation and a letter of endorsement from an existing MB. The examination is conducted in silence. Results are announced by post, within sixty days, in handwritten correspondence from the examining panel.
