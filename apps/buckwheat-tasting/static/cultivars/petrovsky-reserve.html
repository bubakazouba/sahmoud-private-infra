Domaine de Petrovsky Reserve

2020 Vintage  ·  Saratov Oblast, Volga Basin  ·  Single-Origin Estate
Reviewed by Henrik Solberg-Ostrowski MB  ·  Buckwheat Spectator, September 2021

Critical Assessment

Nose:

The Petrovsky Reserve arrives like the opening bars of a Shostakovich quartet: dark, unhurried, and freighted with the accumulated weight of a particular geography. Black loam of the Saratov chernozem — that almost mythological soil of the Russian interior — dominates the first impression, dense and mineral and alive with the specific character of the Volga's flood plain. Toasted rye follows, then faint smoke from the traditional Russian kiln-drying method that the Petrovsky estate has maintained without modification since its founding in 1887. This is a nose of extraordinary historical continuity.

Palate:

Profound mineral weight that exceeds any Western cultivar this critic has evaluated. The Petrovsky 2020 settles onto the palate like a judicial pronouncement: dark crust, aged leather with a thread of fennel seed, and a mid-palate iron concentration that suggests the geological time-depth of the Volga basin itself. Tremendous length. The finish persists for sixty seconds on the finest examples of this cuvee, resolving on a note of cold river stone and ancient wood smoke that is unlike any other cultivar in this registry.

Overall:

The Petrovsky Reserve 2020 is a cultivar of near-legendary status. To compare it with its Western counterparts is, in some sense, a category error; it operates in an aesthetic register of its own devising. A necessary acquisition for any serious collection.

Provenance

The Petrovsky estate was founded in 1887 by Alexandr Petrovsky, a former grain merchant who recognised the singular quality of the Saratov chernozem for buckwheat cultivation. The estate passed through Soviet collectivisation and was returned to Petrovsky family control in 1992; the current director, Dr. Irina Petrovsky-Morozova, holds a doctorate in soil science from Moscow State University and has overseen the estate's return to pre-Soviet cultivation practices. The Reserve program selects from the lowest-elevation plots, where the alluvial mineral concentration is highest. Annual production: 1,200 kg.

Pairings

The Petrovsky Reserve demands the company of its geographic compatriots. Beluga caviar on buckwheat blini is not merely a pairing but an argument: these two produce of the same river basin achieve, together, a harmony that transcends either in isolation. Smoked sturgeon. Cold vodka of exceptional quality, consumed in a single, considered measure. Those who prefer a Western table may find that aged Gruyere (24-month) and black bread provide adequate, if necessarily provincial, companionship.

Cellar Notes

The Petrovsky Reserve is packaged in hand-thrown ceramic tins with fired enamel lids bearing the estate crest — a depiction of the Volga at Saratov. Store at 6-12°C, in conditions of moderate humidity. The ceramic packaging provides exceptional protection against aromatic contamination. Optimal window: 6-18 months. This cultivar has shown no meaningful decline at 24 months under proper conditions.
