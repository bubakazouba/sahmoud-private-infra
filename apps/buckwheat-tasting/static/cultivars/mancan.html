Domaine Mancan Reserve

2019 Vintage  ·  Allegheny Plateau, Pennsylvania  ·  Single-Origin Estate
Reviewed by Margaux Estienne-Beauvoir  ·  Buckwheat Spectator, December 2019

Critical Assessment

Nose:

The nose announces itself with the deliberate confidence of a cultivar that knows precisely what it is. Roasted hazelnut dominates the initial impression — not the aggressive toastiness of lesser origins, but a warm, rounded nuttiness that speaks of unhurried kiln-drying on the Allegheny ridge. Behind it, a precise sliver of dried apricot emerges with patience, persistent and elusive in equal measure. Beeswax notes of uncommon warmth thread through the composition, suggesting a harvest of exceptional ripeness. Swirl once and the minerality announces itself: iron filings, wet limestone, the particular perfume of Pennsylvania clay after October rain.

Palate:

The palate is monumental. Assertive nuttiness yields immediately to a mid-palate of malted earth and iron-rich mineral depth that few cultivars from any appellation can match. There is no diffidence here, no apologetic soft-pedalling of the Allegheny character: this is groat as intellectual proposition, demanding and rewarding in equal measure. The finish extends beyond forty seconds on the longest examples of this cuvee — grey slate, toasted barley, the faintest whisper of pipe tobacco — resolving with a precision that borders on the surgical.

Overall:

Unambiguously transcendent. A reference-point cultivar for the Allegheny appellation and, by extension, for the discipline as a whole. One pours slowly, and says nothing. Cellaring potential: 12 to 18 months in cool, dark, humidity-controlled conditions.

Provenance

The Mancan variety was developed at the Pennsylvania State University Agricultural Experiment Station in 1959 and has since become synonymous with the Allegheny Plateau appellation. The Domaine Mancan Reserve program was established in 2004 by the Howarth-Delacroix family, who farm 340 acres of limestone-rich upland at elevations between 1,800 and 2,200 feet. The 2019 harvest benefited from a dry September with significant diurnal temperature variation, producing groats of exceptional density and mineral concentration. Milling occurs on-site, in a restored 1938 stone mill; the flour is cold-stored at 4°C within six hours of milling.

Pairings

The 2019 Mancan Reserve demands its counterparts be chosen with equivalent seriousness. Cured roe on blini — ideally Sevruga, never Salmon — provides the saline counterpoint that allows the mineral depth to fully express. Aged Comté (36-month minimum) echoes the nutty complexity without competition. A considered silence at the table is, in the view of this critic, the appropriate beverage accompaniment. If one insists on a drink, a dry Fino Sherry, served at cellar temperature in a copita, has proven companionable.

Cellar Notes

Store sealed in opaque linen-lined tins at 10-13°C, 55-65% relative humidity, away from light and vibration. Do not refrigerate. Optimal consumption window: 3-9 months post-milling. The 2019 reserve shows no signs of decline at 24 months; a remarkable result for an unoaked, unpreserved groat.
