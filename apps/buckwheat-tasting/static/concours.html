About the Competition

The Premier Competition

Now in its 38th year, the Concours International de Fagopyrum is the foremost competitive forum for buckwheat producers. Three days of blind tastings before a panel of 18 Master Buckwheat Sommeliers culminate in the awarding of the Grand Prix Estienne-Beauvoir, the field's highest distinction. The competition accepts entries from producers across all recognised appellations; each cultivar is assessed blind, in controlled conditions of 12°C and 55% relative humidity, by a panel that has no knowledge of the producer, appellation, or price point of any entry under evaluation. The integrity of the blind process is, in the view of the Competition Committee, the source of whatever authority the Concours's awards command.

The 2026 edition will be held at the Estienne-Beauvoir Estate, Upper Ojai, California — the first time the competition has been held on North American soil. The choice of the California estate acknowledges the appellation's growing claim to first-tier status and provides the judges' panel with the rare opportunity to evaluate Western Hemisphere cultivars in their terroir of origin.

Competition Categories

Entry Categories 2026

I. Estate Cultivar
Open to any recognised appellation
Single-variety cultivars from estates producing no more than 5,000kg annually. The flagship category; the Grand Prix has been awarded from this category in six of the past eight years.
II. Heritage Single-Origin
Pre-1970 variety heritage designation required
Cultivars produced from varieties with documented cultivation history predating 1970. Pennquad, Mancan, Manor, Common Gray, and equivalent heritage varieties eligible. Supporting documentation required with submission.
III. Experimental Blend
Open category
Blends of two or more varieties, including cross-appellation blends, assessed without prejudice to compositional origin. The most controversial category; the panel's evaluation criteria are reviewed at the start of each edition.
IV. Tartary Class
Fagopyrum tataricum only
Tartary buckwheat, with its markedly higher rutin content and distinct aromatic profile, is assessed under modified criteria developed specifically for the variety. Japanese and Chinese producers have dominated this category since its introduction.
V. Sparkling Buckwheat
Effervescent preparations
The Competition accepts sparkling buckwheat preparations produced by secondary fermentation or controlled carbonation. This category was introduced in 2021 at the Asia-Pacific bureau's recommendation; Niigata producers have submitted the majority of notable entries.
VI. Cellar-Aged (3+ Years)
Minimum 36 months from milling date
The most technically demanding category. Groats must have been maintained in documented storage conditions for a minimum of 36 months. Production date and storage records verified by the Competition Committee prior to acceptance.
Prize Structure

Awards & Distinctions

Grand Prix Estienne-Beauvoir
$25,000

The competition's supreme distinction. Awarded to the single cultivar judged to represent the highest achievement in buckwheat production across all categories and appellations. Recipients also receive a 200-litre estate-aged barrel from the Estienne-Beauvoir Estate cellar and a five-year automatic subscription to the Buckwheat Spectator Patron tier (value $6,000). The Grand Prix has been awarded to producers from the Allegheny Plateau (four times), the Volga Basin (two times), and Brittany (once) in the past seven editions. No Japanese cultivar has yet prevailed, a situation the Asia-Pacific bureau views with increasing impatience.

Gold Medal — Per Category
$5,000

Awarded to the highest-scoring entry in each of the six categories. Categories without an entry of sufficient quality (defined as a panel average below 92 points) may receive no Gold Medal at the Competition Committee's discretion. The Gold Medal is accompanied by a certificate signed by all 18 panel judges and a featured review in the following issue of the Buckwheat Spectator.

Silver Medal — Per Category
$2,000

Awarded to the second-highest scoring entry per category, subject to a minimum panel average of 89 points. Certificate and notification to the Buckwheat Spectator critic who covers the producing appellation.

Bronze Medal — Per Category
$500

Awarded to the third-highest scoring entry per category, subject to a minimum panel average of 86 points. Certificate. Inclusion in the Buckwheat Spectator's annual producer directory.

Judges Panel

2026 Judging Panel

Margaux Estienne-Beauvoir
Competition Director · Master of Buckwheat

Chairs the Competition Committee and serves as the final arbiter in all procedural disputes. Does not vote in category awards to preserve the integrity of the blind process; casts the deciding vote only in the event of a Grand Prix tie.

Henrik Solberg-Ostrowski
Chief Evaluator · Master of Buckwheat

Returns for his twelfth consecutive year on the judging panel. His scores on Eastern European cultivars are considered the panel's definitive assessments; his scoring on California and Japanese cultivars has been a matter of productive controversy.

Yuki Nakamura-Estienne
Asia-Pacific Evaluator · Master of Buckwheat

Returns for her eighth consecutive year. Her assessment of the Tartary and Sparkling categories has been the decisive factor in both category Gold Medal decisions of the past four editions. She has publicly stated her intention to see a Japanese cultivar awarded the Grand Prix before her retirement from the panel.

Dr. Irina Petrovsky-Morozova
Guest Judge · Holder, Dijon MB · Director, Petrovsky Estate

The director of the Petrovsky Estate recuses herself from the evaluation of any Volga Basin cultivar but brings to the panel an expertise in Tartary and Heritage categories unmatched by any other current member. Her participation is a first for a producing estate director; the Competition Committee has approved her participation after a formal conflict-of-interest review.

Francois-Xavier Morin-Duhamel
Breton Evaluator · Buckwheat Professional

Joins the panel as a full evaluator for the first time, having served as an alternate in the previous three editions. His recusal from Breton entries is noted and managed; he contributes most substantially to the Estate Cultivar and Heritage categories from North American appellations.

Dr. Kenji Watanabe-Solberg
Guest Judge · Hokkaido Groat Academy

The Director of Research at the Hokkaido Groat Academy joins the panel for the first time, representing the Academy's formal engagement with the Concours process. His expertise in the sensory chemistry of Japanese cultivars brings a methodological precision that the panel expects to find productive.

The full panel of 18 judges includes twelve additional Master Buckwheat Sommeliers drawn from the Institut Fagopyrum's international network. Full panel roster published in the competition programme.
