Featured Cultivar of the Month

Domaine Mancan Reserve 2019

ALLEGHENY PLATEAU, PENNSYLVANIA  ·  SCORE: 98 PTS  ·  ESTIENNE-BEAUVOIR SCALE

98
POINTS
LEGENDARY

Nose: Roasted hazelnut, warm beeswax, and a precise sliver of dried apricot — elusive and persistent. The aromatic complexity unfolds over three to four minutes in the vessel, rewarding patience with successive layers of dried meadow grass and faint volcanic mineral.

Palate: Assertive nuttiness yields to malted earth and iron-rich mineral depth; the finish lingers for over forty seconds with notes of toasted barley and grey slate. A cultivar of uncommon intellectual rigour.

"One of the most significant groats of the decade. This critic recommends unhurried consumption in considered company — or alone, beside a south-facing window."  —  Margaux Estienne-Beauvoir
