Sample Examination Questions

The Standard of Knowledge

The following questions are drawn from past Associate Buckwheat Sommelier and Buckwheat Professional examinations, reproduced with permission of the Institut Fagopyrum. They are offered to illustrate the level of knowledge expected at each tier.

ABS Level

Identify and evaluate, by blind tasting, three cultivars presented to you in sequence. For each, provide a written tasting note of no fewer than 80 words employing the Estienne-Beauvoir Aroma Wheel vocabulary. Identify the probable appellation of origin for each, and provide a score on the 100-point scale. (40 points; each cultivar assessed separately)
Distinguish, with reference to soil science, the principal mineral differences between the Allegheny Plateau's Devonian limestone geology and the Volga Basin's chernozem. How do these differences manifest in the cultivar's aromatic profile? (20 points)
A guest at your table presents a Mancan Reserve 2019 and requests a cheese pairing. You have access to a 24-month Comté, an 18-month Manchego, a Stilton, and a 3-month Brie. Select and justify your choice. What would you not serve, and why? (15 points)

BP Level

A producer from the Niigata appellation has submitted a cultivar for assessment that displays an unusually high mineral density inconsistent with the Uonuma basin's typical alluvial terroir profile. Propose three hypotheses for this anomaly, each grounded in agronomy or soil science, and describe the investigative protocol you would apply to test them. (35 points)
Trace the development of the French buckwheat tradition from its documented introduction to Brittany in the 13th century to the codification of the Finistere appellation by the Institut Fagopyrum in 1998. Your essay should address the cultural, agricultural, and critical factors that contributed to the establishment of the Grand Cru designation. (40 points)

MB Level (illustrative only)

You are presented with forty cultivars, blind, over three consecutive days. You are provided with water, unsalted crackers, and a palate-cleansing solution of room-temperature chamomile tea. You have 12 minutes per cultivar. The examination is conducted in silence. No notes may be brought in; notes taken during the evaluation will be collected at the end of each session. (The complete examination; results announced by post within 60 days)

Teaching Faculty

The Certification Faculty

Margaux Estienne-Beauvoir
Master of Buckwheat, Hon. PhD · Director, Institut Fagopyrum

The founding figure of modern buckwheat criticism, Margaux Estienne-Beauvoir holds the MB designation conferred at the inaugural examination in 1991 and has sat on the examining panel at every subsequent MB examination. Her monograph on the Estienne-Beauvoir Scoring System (2019) is considered the definitive statement of the discipline's epistemological foundations. She teaches the capstone seminar of the BP programme at Dijon and conducts the oral examination of every MB candidate personally.

Henrik Solberg-Ostrowski
Master of Buckwheat · Eastern European Affairs

Solberg-Ostrowski holds the Norwegian doctorate in agricultural science and the MB designation; he is the only holder of both distinctions. His research on the mineral expression of chernozem soils, published in 2014, fundamentally revised the field's understanding of Volga Basin terroir. At the Institut Fagopyrum, he teaches the soil science modules of the ABS and BP programmes and serves as the Eastern European assessor on the MB examining panel.

Yuki Nakamura-Estienne
Master of Buckwheat, Asia-Pacific Bureau Chief

Born in Kyoto, trained at the Hokkaido Groat Academy, and further distinguished by a period of study at the Institut Fagopyrum, Nakamura-Estienne is the primary voice of the Japanese buckwheat tradition in the international critical community. Her 2018 monograph on the Japanese buckwheat tradition is required reading for all BP candidates. She teaches the Japan module at Sapporo and serves as the Asia-Pacific assessor on the MB panel.

Anastasia Volkov-Brennan
Associate Buckwheat Sommelier · Volga Basin Correspondent

Volkov-Brennan holds the ABS distinction with the highest score recorded in the 2011 examination and has been offered the BP curriculum three times; she has declined each time on the grounds that the practical demands of the Volga Basin field season take precedence over examination preparation. Her correspondence from the Saratov and Tambov growing regions constitutes the most reliable field reporting available on Russian buckwheat production. She teaches the Volga Basin module of the ABS programme at Dijon.

Patrick Czernowitz-Hale
Associate Buckwheat Sommelier · North American Field Editor

Czernowitz-Hale covers the North American appellations with a diligence and precision that have made his quarterly reports the standard reference for the Allegheny and Pennsylvania Dutch Country growing regions. He holds the ABS from the Penn State Extension programme's inaugural cohort in 2009 and teaches the North American module at the UC Davis and Penn State locations. His annotated bibliography of Allegheny buckwheat research is maintained on the Institut Fagopyrum's digital archive.
