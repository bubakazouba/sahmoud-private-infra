Year-by-Year Retrospective

Critical Summaries

2019 — The Allegheny Apotheosis

There will be, in the long view of buckwheat history, a 2019 and a before-2019. The conditions of that growing season on the Allegheny Plateau — a dry, warm July followed by a September of extraordinary temperature variation — produced a concentration of iron-rich mineral and nutty aromatic complexity that no subsequent harvest has replicated. The Mancan Reserve 2019, at 98 points, stands as the finest buckwheat cultivar assessed by this journal in its 38-year history. Connoisseurs who secured allocations of the estate-bottled production have, in the view of this panel, acquired the equivalent of a First Growth. Those who did not should console themselves with the knowledge that excellent cultivars from this year remain available from the Manor, Horizon, and Pennquad estates at equitable prices.

2020 — The Year of the Volga

While the American and European press focused on pandemic-related disruptions to the 2020 harvest, the Volga Basin continued its implacable self-expression with the quiet confidence of a region accustomed to operating beyond the notice of its peers. The Petrovsky Reserve 2020, assessed by this journal at 97 points, represents the finest expression of the Saratov chernozem's potential that we have encountered. The mineral density, the extraordinary length of finish, the particular leather-and-fennel character of the mid-palate — these are qualities that cannot be manufactured by human ingenuity; they are the direct expression of a soil of unparalleled fertility and a tradition of cultivation that has persisted through empires, revolutions, and the indifferent attentions of agricultural economists.

2021 — The Hokkaido Ascent

The Koto Single-Origin Estate 2021, at 95 points, established beyond reasonable contestation that Hokkaido is a first-tier appellation deserving of comparison with the finest Continental and American producers. The volcanic pumice loam of the central plateau, fed by snowmelt of remarkable purity, achieved in 2021 a mineral concentration and aromatic delicacy that exceeded all previous benchmarks for the appellation. The Allegheny Plateau also delivered outstanding results; the simultaneous excellence of these two geographically and philosophically distinct regions is, in the view of this critic, one of the more compelling arguments for buckwheat's status as a truly global discipline.
