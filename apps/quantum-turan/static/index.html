2 · Why the magic number is 6

Every subspace and its orthogonal complement split the whole matrix space: dim V + dim V⊥ = n². So proving "V is small" is the same as proving "its complement is big."

Mn(ℂ)  —  total dimension n²
V  (≤ n²−6)
V⊥ ≥ 6
The entire upper bound is a hunt for 6 dimensions. "dim V ≤ n²−6" ⟺ "dim V⊥ ≥ 6". The proof never touches V directly again — it works entirely inside the small complement W = V⊥ and shows it can't be squeezed below 6.

3 · Cliques and "covering"

A quantum (n−1)-clique is a rank-(n−1) projection Pe = I − ee* that "sees" the maximum (n−1)² dimensions of V under compression. The clever move: not having one is a positive statement about W.

Covering condition Cov(W)
What "killed" means
For every direction e ≠ 0
there is a nonzero A ∈ W with
A e = 0  and  A* e = 0
The key equivalence (§4)
V has no (n−1)-clique  ⟺  V⊥ covers every direction
Engine of the equivalence: the compression by Pe has range exactly (n−1)² (a clean rank-nullity count, §3). A clique would fill that range entirely, leaving no room for a covering element — and vice versa. Proved both directions.

5 · The heart: why 5 dimensions can't cover

Assume Wh is traceless Hermitian, dimℝ ≤ 5, and (for contradiction) that it covers. Split into cases on n — every branch dead-ends.

  • n = 3 → Weaver's base case axiom → covering fails
  • n ≥ 4, dim ≤ 4 → incidence variety has dim ≤ 2n−1, too small to hit every direction → fails
  • n ≥ 4, dim = 5 → rank forcing: every H must have rank ≤ 2
    • bounded-rank dichotomy axiom ⟹ one of:
      • common block → fails (Lemma 6.7)
      • common vector → fails (Lemma 6.8)
every branch ⟹ covering fails ⟹ contradiction ⟹ dimℝ Wh ≥ 6 ✓
The skew-symmetric escape hatch is closed: the general bounded-rank theorem has a third "primitive" type (3×3 skew-symmetric), but skew (A*=−A) can never be Hermitian (A*=A), so only block / vector survive — and both are killed below.

6 · The two ways covering dies

Both surviving structures have an obvious direction they can't kill.

Common block — all mass in a 2×2 corner
Pick a direction e living in the empty coordinates (rows 3…n). Then He = 0 forces H = 0 — there's nothing in those columns to use. Can't cover those e.
Common vector — everything built from one f
Test the very vector f it's built from. Since g ⊥ f, the equation g + cf = 0 forces c = 0 and g = 0, so H = 0. Can't cover e = f.
Same punchline, twice: each structure leaves one specific direction with no nonzero matrix to kill it, so the covering condition collapses — exactly the contradiction the proof needs.

9 · The compression count, as an exact sequence (§3)

Why is dim range(ΦPe) exactly (n−1)²? Build a short exact sequence and read off the dimensions.

Exactness does the counting: ker L = range(ΦPe) and im L = ker g. Since g is onto ℂ, im L has dim 2n−1, so ker L has dim n²−(2n−1) = (n−1)². The alternating dimensions n² − 2n + 1 collapse to (n−1)².

10 · Where compression lives, and how the witness self-destructs (§4)

Mn(ℂ) splits as V ⊕ W. The compression of V sits inside range(ΦPe); a covering witness in W is forced to be both inside and orthogonal to it.

The pivot of §4: a clique would make PeV Pe fill the entire (n−1)²-dim range. Any covering witness is fixed by the compression, so it lands in that range — but it's also orthogonal to it. Non-degeneracy of ⟨·,·⟩ then kills it. Hence "no clique ⟺ covering."

11 · The real-ification isomorphism (§5)

Split every A ∈ W into Hermitian + i·(Hermitian). That's a real-linear isomorphism — and it pins the dimensions together.

Why it matters: the final obstruction is real algebraic geometry, so we need real objects. φ turns one complex space into a pair of real Hermitian ones without losing dimension — keeping the "hunt for 6" honest across the change of field.

12 · Evaluation, kernels, and the incidence variety (§6)

Covering is exactly: "every direction has a witness." Package all (witness, direction) pairs into one geometric object and project it two ways.

The dimension squeeze: the fiber of π₂ over a direction [e] is the projectivized kernel ker(eve)∩Wh. For small Wh the whole incidence variety I is too low-dimensional for π₂ to hit every direction in ℂPn−1 — so some direction has no witness and covering fails. Combined with the rank-2 forcing + the block/vector dichotomy, this closes the n ≥ 4 cases.
