What is this dashboard?

Sandbox for exploring how to fund ~$80k/yr of living expenses during low-income grad-school years (masters + PhD) with minimum federal tax. Not a committed plan. Uses real Vanguard 166-lot + AMZN 5-lot data as of 2026-05-08.

SpecID / HIFO You pick which lots to sell — modeled here as highest-cost-basis first, which minimizes gain recognized per dollar of proceeds.
LTCG bracket-fill (harvest) In a low-income year the 0% long-term capital gains bracket may cover $40k+ of realized gains. "Harvesting" means intentionally selling appreciated lots to use up that bracket — resetting cost basis higher for free, then re-buying. No wash-sale rule applies to gains.
Roth conversion ladder Moving money from a traditional 401k/IRA to Roth IRA while ordinary income is low. Counts as ordinary income now, grows tax-free forever. Each dollar converted shrinks the 0%-LTCG headroom.
AMZN ST→LT aging AMZN lots held less than 1 year are short-term (taxed as ordinary income at 22-37%). The Oct-2025 lot crosses to LT on 2026-10-15; the Apr-2026 lot on 2027-04-15. Selling before that crossing is expensive.
Delta (savings vs baseline) The difference between your strategy's cumulative tax and the baseline (FIFO sell at average basis ratio, no optimization). A positive delta means you saved that much in taxes.
Annual spend i Total annual living expenses funded from portfolio sales. This entire amount is modeled as coming from selling LT lots (long-term capital gains), not from ordinary income. $80,000
Years to model i How many years of grad school / post-Amazon life to model. Default 7 = 2 masters + 5 PhD. Each year is treated as a separate tax year. 7
PhD stipend / yr i NSF GRFP stipend = $37,000/yr for FY2026. Counts as ordinary income (taxable). Set to $0 for masters years when no stipend exists. The slider applies the same stipend to all modeled years — adjust per-year in a future version. $37,000
Roth conversion / yr i Roth conversion = moving money from traditional 401k/IRA to Roth IRA. It counts as ordinary income. CRITICAL: Roth conversion uses up 0%-LTCG headroom dollar-for-dollar — more conversion means more LTCG gets taxed at 15% instead of 0%. The default fills the remaining 0%-LTCG headroom after stipend. $0
Year 1 (leaves Amazon) i The first year modeled — when he stops earning Amazon salary. AMZN lot ST/LT aging is computed relative to this year. Default 2027. 2027
⚠ Average Cost is only available for mutual fund holdings at Vanguard — not ETFs (your VTI/VXUS/QQQ are ETFs). Shown for comparison purposes only; you cannot elect this method at the brokerage for these positions.

Year-by-year federal tax — strategy vs baseline i Blue = your strategy (HIFO/SpecID + Roth conversion plan). Gray = baseline (selling random mix at average basis ratio, no optimization). CA tax excluded from this chart for clarity — see table.

Blue bars = tax under the chosen strategy. Gray bars = baseline (FIFO sell at average portfolio basis ratio, no optimization). The gap between gray and blue is the delta — tax you avoid each year by being deliberate about which lots you sell.

Cumulative LTCG harvested vs cumulative tax i LTCG harvested = total long-term capital gains you realized to fund spending. Cumulative tax = total tax paid (fed + CA). The gap between them is money you kept. A flat tax line vs rising LTCG line = efficient harvesting.

Harvest = intentionally realizing long-term capital gains in a low-income year to reset your cost basis higher — for free, because gains within the 0% LTCG bracket incur no federal tax. You immediately re-buy the same shares (no wash-sale rule on gains). The blue line shows cumulative gains harvested; the green line shows cumulative tax paid. A wide gap between them means you're pulling gains into your portfolio at minimal tax cost. The dashed line shows what baseline tax would have been.

Per-year tax savings vs baseline i Delta per year = (baseline tax) minus (strategy tax). Positive = you saved money vs doing nothing. Baseline = selling at the average portfolio basis ratio with no lot selection. This is the per-year version of the overall lifetime savings shown at the top.

Delta = strategy tax subtracted from baseline tax. Computed as: (fed + CA tax if you sold at average portfolio basis ratio with no optimization) minus (fed + CA tax under the chosen method). Green bar = you paid less than baseline. The lifetime total of all these bars = the "Lifetime tax savings" callout at the top.

LTCG headroom used vs available (0% rate zone) i 0%-LTCG headroom = $49,450 (single 2026, IRS Rev. Proc. 2025-32) minus your ordinary taxable income (stipend + Roth conversion minus std deduction). LTCG below headroom is tax-free federally. LTCG above headroom is taxed at 15% (up to $545,500). CA taxes all LTCG as ordinary income regardless.

The 0% federal LTCG bracket runs up to $49,450 of total taxable income (single, 2026 — IRS Rev. Proc. 2025-32). Your ordinary income (stipend + Roth conversion minus standard deduction) occupies the bottom of that bracket; what remains is "headroom" you can fill with harvested gains at 0% federal rate. Blue = available headroom, green = gains realized. Ideal: green stays at or below blue each year.
